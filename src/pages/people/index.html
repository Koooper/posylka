The people who never touch your parcel

For every person who carries a box, several others keep the system running: answering calls, watching the sky, defending the servers, keeping the lights on and looking after the staff themselves. Open a card to spend a day with each team.

Each day is a composite built from published accounts of how these teams work. Internal tools and timings are illustrative.
