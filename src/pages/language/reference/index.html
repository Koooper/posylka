Names in two alphabets

This page introduces each Ukrainian name in Cyrillic first, then uses the Latin spelling. The Latin forms follow Ukraine’s official 2010 transliteration system, which is what you’ll see on most passports and road signs. People may choose their own spellings, so always check how someone writes their name.

Галина Halyna

г is h, not g

Микола Mykola

и is y; і is i

Андрій Andrii

й is i (y at the start of a word)

Юрій Yurii

ю is yu at the start, iu elsewhere

Тетяна Tetiana

я is ya at the start, ia elsewhere

Київ Kyiv

ї is yi at the start, i elsewhere

Харків Kharkiv

х is kh

Ольга Olha

the soft sign ь is left out
