Де моя посилка?

Choose a parcel. Follow its journey.

Нова пошта (Nova Poshta, literally “New Post”) does business in English as Nova Post, part of the NOVA group. It is the private delivery company most Ukrainians rely on. Since Russia’s full-scale invasion in February 2022 it has kept parcels moving through blackouts, air-raid alerts and missile strikes. Track five parcels and meet the people who move them.

“Де моя посилка?” means “Where is my parcel?” It may be the most-typed question in Ukraine.

Interactive educational simulation. Five fictional parcels; no live tracking. Messages, timings and app screens are illustrative.

The journey

All times are Kyiv time. Each update is one moment in the parcel’s trip. Switch between what the customer sees and what the staff are doing. Tap any to learn it.

Pick a parcel from “Choose a story” above to start tracking. Each takes a different route: across the country, across a border, across an ocean, from a US warehouse to a city 30 km from Russia, and from a US lab to a workshop at the front.

The people in these stories are composites. The rules, prices, procedures and events come from published reporting and company statements up to October 2026; see the source library.
